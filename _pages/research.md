My research develops **AI-powered transportation systems** that are safe, efficient, stable, and trustworthy.

I work at the intersection of traffic flow theory, control, artificial intelligence, and intelligent transportation systems. My current research focuses on connected and automated vehicles, traffic flow modeling and control, human-centered mobility, transportation cybersecurity, and trustworthy AI for future mobility systems.

## Research Areas

* AI for intelligent transportation systems
* Traffic flow theory, modeling, and control
* Connected and automated vehicles
* Cybersecurity and resilient mobility systems
* Human-aware and socially adaptive mobility
